# Rules

- Every table plays for chips. Nothing is made or lost, only moved.
- You start with 1,000. Sitting down costs the table's stack.
- Stacks carry from hand to hand. The blinds switch each hand.
- Hit zero and you lose the seat. You can still watch, or buy back in.
- Leave between hands to take your stack home.
- No one sees cards that aren't theirs. No one rewinds a hand.
